Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026.

On-device data

PokerCompare stores comparison history, practice progress, language choice, sound and haptic preferences, advertising counters, temporary ad-free expiry, and a locally verified remove-ads entitlement on your device. There is no account and no server-operated cloud sync.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. Depending on consent and platform settings, these SDKs may process device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction, advertising data, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics for advertising and analytics. The app does not send selected cards or hand contents in advertising or analytics events.

Where required, the app asks for consent before requesting ads. Tracking permission is optional; refusing it does not restrict core features. A verified remove-ads purchaser does not initialize or request ads.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional non-consumable purchase. The app reads verified StoreKit entitlements but does not receive payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

Comparison history is limited to the latest 100 records and practice history to the latest 10 sets. You can clear both categories in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to system backups.

Children and gambling

The app provides educational rules and comparison tools only. It offers no betting, prizes, virtual currency, real-money play, or online games.
