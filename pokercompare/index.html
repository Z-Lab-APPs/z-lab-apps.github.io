What the app does

Compare two Texas Hold'em or five-card hands, inspect each best five-card hand, study all nine hand ranks, and practice with ten-question sets. English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese are included.

Free core features

All comparisons, lessons, practice, history, and sharing remain free without usage limits. A one-time optional purchase only removes advertising.
