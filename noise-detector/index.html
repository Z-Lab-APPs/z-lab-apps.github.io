Noise Detector · 噪音检测器

Tap Start Measurement and begin immediately after a short countdown. When finished, optionally save a name, address, current-location lookup, photos, notes, conditions, and estimated dBA history on your iPhone. Every save detail can be left blank.

Important accuracy limit

Every reading is an estimate. An iPhone is not an IEC 61672 certified sound level meter. Uncalibrated readings are best for relative comparisons using the same phone and placement.

Ads and purchase

The free version shows a bottom banner that does not cover controls and may show an interstitial before saving and whenever History is entered. Offline use, no fill, timeout, or ad errors never block saving or viewing local history. A one-time US$0.99 purchase permanently removes ads.

Optional location

Location is requested only after you tap Use Current Location while saving. Apple geocoding returns editable address text; raw coordinates are not retained. You can decline, type an address, or leave it blank.
