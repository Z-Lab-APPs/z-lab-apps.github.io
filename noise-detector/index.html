Noise Detector · 噪音检测器

Local-first estimated dBA records for comparing rooms and housing conditions. Save property names, addresses, notes, selected photos, and measurement history on your iPhone.

Important accuracy limit

Every reading is an estimate. An iPhone is not an IEC 61672 certified sound level meter. Uncalibrated readings are best for relative comparisons using the same phone and placement.

Ads and purchase

The free version may show banner ads on History and Settings only. Measurement preparation, active measurement, and result saving have no ads. A one-time purchase permanently removes ads.
