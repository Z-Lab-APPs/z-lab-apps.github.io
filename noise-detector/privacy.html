Noise Detector Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

English

Noise Detector uses microphone access only while you actively run a measurement. Audio samples are processed in memory to calculate estimated sound-level metrics and are not written to disk, uploaded, used for advertising, or used for analytics.

Names, optional address text, measurement conditions, event markers, calibration profiles, estimated metrics, selected photos, and downsampled level trends are stored locally on your device. They leave the device only when you explicitly share a CSV or text summary.

Location is never requested automatically. If you tap Use Current Location while saving, the app requests When In Use permission and sends the current location to Apple’s geocoding service to obtain editable address text. Noise Detector does not retain raw latitude or longitude. You may decline, type an address, or leave the address blank; saving remains available.

The app has no account, cloud sync, or runtime AI. You may choose individual photos through the system picker; compressed copies remain local. The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and UMP for a bottom banner plus interstitials before saving and when entering History. Depending on consent and platform settings, Google may process device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction, advertising data, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics. Device identifiers may be used for tracking where consent and platform permission allow it. After UMP consent, production ads may request App Tracking Transparency permission; declining does not limit core features. An ad timeout, no fill, offline state, or error never blocks a local save or History access. A verified one-time purchase disables ad SDK initialization and requests. You can delete all local data in Settings.

名称、可选地址文字、测量条件、事件标记、校准档案、估算指标、用户选择的图片和降采样声级趋势仅保存在你的设备上。只有你主动分享 CSV 或文本摘要时，相关导出内容才会离开设备。
