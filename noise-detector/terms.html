Noise Detector Terms

Effective: 1 October 2026

Estimated readings

Noise Detector provides estimated dBA values for personal comparison. It is not a certified sound level meter and must not be used for legal evidence, occupational safety, medical diagnosis, enforcement, or certified hearing-protection decisions.

Local records

You control the property names, addresses, notes, photos, measurements, and exports stored on your device. Keep your own backup of information you need. Deleting the app or using Clear All Local Data can remove these records.

The free version may show banner ads on History and Settings. A non-consumable in-app purchase removes ads for the Apple Account that purchased it, subject to App Store availability and verification.
