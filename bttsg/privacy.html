Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated 24 September 2026

English

BTT Singapore stores study progress, mistakes, bookmarks, settings and mock-exam history locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not collect your name or email.

A production release uses Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Depending on your region, consent and ATT choices, these services may process device identifiers, product interaction, advertising data, coarse location, and crash, performance and other diagnostic data. Device identifiers may be used for cross-app tracking and personalised advertising only when the required permission and consent have been obtained. Declining tracking does not restrict any study feature.

Analytics events cover feature use, question category and whether an answer was correct; they do not contain question text, the selected option, your name or your email. Purchases are processed by Apple. Third-party processing and retention are governed by the providers' policies and your consent choices.

You can revisit privacy choices and reset learning data in Settings. Contact: zzzhhhaaaiiii@gmail.com.
