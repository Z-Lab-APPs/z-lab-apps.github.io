English

PhotoJigsaw is an offline-first puzzle app with no account and no developer-operated server.

Photos and puzzle data

Photos selected through Apple’s system photo picker, cropped images, puzzle state, history, and settings are processed and stored on your device. They are not uploaded by PhotoJigsaw to the developer or to the advertising SDK. Deleting a project removes its unreferenced local image; clearing app data removes local projects.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Depending on consent, region, device settings, and Google’s current SDK behavior, these services may process device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction, advertising data, performance data, crash data, and other diagnostic data for third-party advertising, developer advertising, analytics, and consent functionality. If tracking authorization is requested, you may decline without losing core functionality. Privacy choices can be revisited from Settings when required.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional non-consumable remove-ads purchase. PhotoJigsaw reads verified StoreKit entitlements but does not receive full payment-card details.

Retention and children

Local puzzle data remains until you delete projects, clear data, or uninstall the app. Advertising providers retain data under their own policies. PhotoJigsaw is not directed to children under 13 and is not offered in the Kids category.

Your choices and contact

You can deny tracking, change available privacy options, remove local data, or buy Pro to stop all ad requests. Questions: support@z-lab.app.
