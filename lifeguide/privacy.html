English

Reading in LifeGuide works fully offline; the app needs no account and does not collect your name or email.

The app shows ads with Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and asks for consent with the Google User Messaging Platform. Depending on your region, consent and App Tracking Transparency choices, these services may process device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction, advertising data, and performance and other diagnostic data. Device identifiers are used for cross-app tracking and personalised ads only with the required permission and consent; declining tracking does not limit any reading feature. Where required you can change your choices in More → Ad privacy options. Google's processing is governed by its privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and your consent choices.

Favourites, reading history, search history and text-size settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. External links open in your browser and are governed by those websites' own policies.
